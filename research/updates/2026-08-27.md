# POE2 Update Watch — 2026-08-27

## Officially confirmed

- Current live baseline remains **0.5.4f**. No newer live patch notes/hotfix with gameplay changes were found in the official Early Access Patch Notes index during this check.
- GGG Live remains scheduled for **2026-08-27 PDT** (2026-08-28 03:30 GMT+8) to preview Path of Exile 2 and 0.5.5. At the time of this update, that reveal has not happened yet.

## Data-site verifiable

PoE2DB has opened a dedicated **Version 0.5.5** page and currently records the following as pre-release scope sourced to prior developer discussion/Tavern Talk rather than final patch notes:

- one-month event league with a **fresh economy** that does not replace or wipe Runes of Aldur;
- roughly **two-thirds of the patch** focused on balance changes involving skills, passive tree and uniques;
- planned **Chaos damage** rework;
- planned **Ignite** rework;
- new skills and uniques;
- swords are **not** part of 0.5.5 (they remain 1.0/Duelist content).

PoE2DB separately labels Act 4 replayability, Ranger/Druid third Ascendancies and a death-summary feature as **not confirmed** for 0.5.5.

Source: https://poe2db.tw/Version_0.5.5
