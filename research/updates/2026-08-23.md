# POE2 Knowledge Update — 2026-08-23

## Officially confirmed / developer-confirmed

- 0.5.5 is planned to include a roughly one-month temporary event with a fresh, separate economy. This was stated by game director Jonathan Rogers in the official 0.5.4 Patch Preview video (YouTube id `OtJGXA6u054`) and has been independently mirrored/transcribed by multiple community sources.
- This does **not** mean the existing Runes of Aldur characters/economy are wiped; the event is a separate fresh-start environment.
- GGG has not yet announced the exact 0.5.5 launch date or the complete event rules. The next official information checkpoints remain the gamescom reveal on 2026-08-25 and GGG Live on 2026-08-27 PDT.

## Data-site / secondary verification

- Multiple current 0.5.5 tracking pages consistently trace the one-month fresh-economy statement back to Jonathan Rogers / the official 0.5.4 Patch Preview.
- No new 0.5.4f hotfix, balance change, skill/item data change, trade-rule change, or endgame-system change was found for 2026-08-22 through the morning of 2026-08-23.

## Community observations

- Community discussion continues to speculate about new ascendancies, weapons, skill balance changes, rewards and the event start date. These remain speculation until the 2026-08-25/27 official reveals.

## Still unverified

- Exact 0.5.5 launch date.
- Exact event duration wording ("roughly one month" vs a fixed number of days).
- Event migration/end-state rules.
- Any exclusive rewards or leaderboard structure.
- Any new class, weapon, ascendancy, skill, unique, passive-tree change, crafting change or Atlas/endgame change.

## Impact on existing knowledge

- **Old conclusion invalidated:** the 2026-08-22 update classified the 0.5.5 fresh economy / temporary event as community speculation. That classification was too conservative.
- **Current conclusion:** `0.5.5 = developer-confirmed temporary fresh-economy event`, while release date and detailed rules remain `NEEDS_VERIFICATION`.
- Current live gameplay baseline remains `0.5.4f`.

## Actionable guidance

- Do not assume current Runes of Aldur wealth will transfer into the 0.5.5 event; plan for a fresh-start economy if participating.
- Do not liquidate or abandon current characters on the assumption they will be deleted; current evidence indicates the event is separate.
- Hold off on build-starter, crafting-stockpile and market speculation until the 2026-08-25/27 official reveals expose actual balance/rule changes.
