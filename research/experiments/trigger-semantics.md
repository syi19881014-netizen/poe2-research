# Trigger 技术语义对照实验

## 目标

建立英文原文条件与实际判定的技术词典。

## 重点短语

- you
- your skills
- when you hit
- when your skills hit
- skills you use yourself
- triggered skills
- minions from supported skills
- totems created by supported skills

## 方法

对每条条件找一个最小化测试案例，移除所有其他可触发来源，仅比较 Player self-use / Trigger / Totem / Minion 四类。
